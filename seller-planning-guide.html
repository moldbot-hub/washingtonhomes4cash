Washington seller preparation

Prepare a damaged Washington house for an as-is offer review

Organize condition, access, and repair information before comparing a purchase proposal for a house that needs substantial work.

Prepared by Washington Home Solutions LLC · Reviewed September 5, 2026

Make a condition inventory before spending on repairs

Walk only areas that are safe and that you are authorized to enter. Group known concerns by roof and exterior, water intrusion, interior finishes, electrical or plumbing concerns, and structural questions. Describe what you observed and when. A stain is an observation; its cause may still need investigation. Do not enter an unsafe building to complete a checklist.

Collect the records you already have

Set aside prior repair invoices, available permit records, contractor estimates, and relevant inspection reports. Note the date and scope of each document. A quote for one repair does not establish the cost of every related problem. If an insurance claim exists, keep its status distinct from the condition description and seek appropriate advice about the claim.

Clarify the property’s current use

Explain whether the house is occupied, vacant, partly usable, or subject to an access limitation. Tell the buyer about contents, stored materials, and any areas that cannot be inspected. If tenants or co-owners are involved, do not assume that a proposed sale date also resolves possession or signing authority.

Compare work assumptions in writing

A direct buyer may evaluate the house differently from a buyer relying on financing or a move-in-ready condition. Ask what inspection or feasibility conditions the offer contains, what happens if new information appears, and which party handles any agreed work. A website cannot establish whether your particular property qualifies for a loan or a purchase.

Decide what information improves the decision

Before paying for cleanup or renovation, identify the decision it would support. You may need a written proposal, a professional inspection, or clarification of a permit issue before choosing a path.
