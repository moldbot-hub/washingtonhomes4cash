Frequently asked questions

Everything Washington homeowners want to know before selling for cash.

How fast can you close?

Timing depends on your agreement, title, payoff information, signing authority and any outstanding conditions. Discuss a workable closing and possession date before deciding.

Are there any fees or commissions?

We do not charge an agent commission as the buyer. Your agreement and settlement statement identify other costs, payoffs and credits. Compare estimated net proceeds before accepting.

Do you buy houses in any condition?

We can review properties needing repairs. Describe known conditions and access limitations; purchase interest depends on the individual property and written terms.

How do you decide your offer?

We look at recent comparable sales near you and the home's condition, then factor in the repairs and costs we'll take on. We're happy to show you the math.

Am I obligated to accept?

Never. Our offer is free and 100% no-obligation. If it's not right for you, no hard feelings.

What if I'm facing foreclosure?

We can often help — sometimes by closing before your sale date, sometimes by pointing you to a better option. See our Foreclosure Help page or just reach out.

Do I need to be moved out?

No. We work around your move and can be flexible on possession after closing if you need a little time.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We never sell your data and only use it to make and discuss your offer.
