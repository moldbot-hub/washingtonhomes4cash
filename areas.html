What helps confirm property fit

  • Give the city or unincorporated area and address so the relevant building or permitting jurisdiction can be identified.
  • For fire or water damage, explain whether occupancy or entry has been restricted and who holds the existing inspection records.
  • For rural or island sites, identify practical access, outbuildings, well or septic systems, and any known damage affecting them.
  • Disclose open permits, correction notices or work already started. Do not assume an as-is sale removes existing responsibilities; ask the appropriate professional.
